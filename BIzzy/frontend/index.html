🆘 Help & Support

Guides below.
💬 Chat on WhatsApp 🎥 Watch help video

🧾 Bill kaise banaye

1. Left menu me New Bill (नया बिल) kholo.
2. Customer chuno — naya customer hai to wahi se naam/phone jod do; bina customer ke "Walk-in" bill bhi ban jata hai.
3. Har line me product khojo (naam likhte hi list aati hai), qty daalo. Rate apne aap sell price se aayega.
4. Discount dena ho to neeche discount daalo. GST on hai to bill me GST apne aap jud jayega.
5. Customer ne kitna diya woh "Paid" me daalo — bacha hua apne aap udhaar ban jayega.
6. Save Bill dabao. Bill banne par WhatsApp button se bill customer ko bhej sakte ho, aur PDF Download se bill ki PDF milegi (WhatsApp par PDF haath se attach karna hota hai).

⏳ Udhaar reminder kaise bheje

1. Customers screen par jao — jiska paisa baki hai uske saamne pending rakam dikhegi.
2. Customer kholo, ya Dashboard/Reports ki "Pending payments" list me Remind dabao.
3. WhatsApp ready-made reminder ke saath khul jayega — bas Send dabana hai. Kuch apne aap nahi bhejta, bhejna tumhare haath me hai.
4. Customer ka phone number na ho to pehle customer ki detail me number jod do, warna reminder nahi khulega.

💾 Backup / Restore kaise kare

1. Settings → Data Backup me jao (sirf owner).
2. App har roz apne aap ek backup snapshot banata hai (aakhri 7 rehte hain). Jab chaaho Backup now se turant snapshot bana sakte ho, aur ⬇ se file download karke pen-drive/email me safe rakh sakte ho.
3. Galti ho jaye to snapshot ke saamne Restore dabao — wohi purana data wapas aa jayega. Restore se pehle app current data ka ek aur snapshot apne aap bana leta hai, taaki restore bhi ulta ho sake.
4. Dhyaan do: restore se staff ke login nahi badalte — sirf dukaan ka data (bill, stock, customer, kharcha) wapas aata hai.

🔄 Subscription renew kaise kare

1. Settings → Subscription & plan me apna plan aur bache hue din dekho.
2. UPI se plan ki rakam DILIGENCE ko bhejo aur UPI / UTR reference note kar lo.
3. Usi screen par plan chuno, rakam aur reference daalkar Submit payment claim dabao.
4. DILIGENCE team verify karke plan chalu kar degi — status wahi screen par "active" ho jayega aur subscription invoice bhi wahi milega.
5. Plan khatm hone par kuch din grace milta hai; uske baad app sirf dekhne (read-only) mode me chala jata hai — purana data hamesha safe rehta hai, renew karte hi sab phir chalu.

📦 Stock / Purchase kaise add kare

1. Naya saaman pehle Products / Stock me jodo — naam, kharid bhaav (buy price), bechne ka bhaav (sell price) aur GST % daalo. Shuru ka stock hai to qty bhi wahi likh do.
2. Supplier se maal aaye to Purchases me purchase banao — supplier chuno, items aur qty daalo; stock apne aap badh jayega.
3. Supplier ko payment do to usi purchase me payment jodo — kitna dena baki hai woh apne aap dikhega.
4. Stock kabhi haath se edit nahi hota — purchase se badhta hai, bill se ghat-ta hai. Isi liye hisaab hamesha sahi rehta hai. Stock kam ho to Dashboard par low-stock alert aayega.
